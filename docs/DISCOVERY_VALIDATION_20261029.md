## Implemented and audited

Owner-configured source pages/feeds are researched on durable business events. Literal need matching preserves the actual source URL, full-response hash, gateway retrieval time, exact observed excerpt, capability/cost assessment, uncertainty and proposed next action. Canonical URL identities, immutable effect IDs, cooldowns, freshness checks, market attempt caps and lifetime conservative allocations prevent duplicate or unbounded work across restarts and reconfiguration.

Identified leads require an exact observed email address and retain existing outreach/opt-out rules. Self-initiated proposals have no customer. Explicit bounded experiments create one private sample through the registered service plan, budget authorization, gateway and scope validator. They record hypotheses, success criteria, deadlines and machine-enforced stops. Explicit attribution can connect a later existing matching-service order; it cannot create a payment or customer.

Outcomes retain actual responses, conversions, revisions, research/prototype/delivery costs and refunds. Commercial evaluation uses its deadline window. Net recognition caps the PayPal service's gross earned metric at unrefunded receipts and subtracts fees and measured costs. Missing records, provisional costs, unknown effects and unpaid promises suppress positive performance. Priority adaptation stays within owner bounds. Free observations use bounded batches and durable fair cursors, including historical completed orders.

The private monochrome operator page shows markets, evidence, experiments, sample inspection, outcomes, recovery, paginated history and upcoming work. Existing session, Origin and CSRF protections apply. Public/customer projections do not gain access to discovery records. Discovery starts disabled.

Audit regressions cover exact contact matching, Unicode evidence spans, original cached retrieval timestamps, evidence visibility before offers, changed budgets/capabilities immediately before dispatch, known local nondispatch versus uncertain provider effects, late conversions, research cost attribution, incomplete outcome coverage, refunds, and bounded fair observation after restart. Service processes also use Python isolated mode so a current-directory package cannot shadow a separately installed service.
